# Code of conduct

exostab adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, welcome newcomers (including students asking basic questions),
criticise ideas rather than people, and keep discussions about the science and the code.

Unacceptable behaviour, such as harassment, insults or publishing someone's private information, can be
reported to the maintainer at mehmet@mehmet-oz.com. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove
contributions or block participants who do not follow this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
