Stability indicators for 1079 multi-planet systems of the NASA Exoplanet Archive (Planetary Systems Composite Parameters, downloaded 2026-11-02), computed 2026-11-02 with exostab 1.0.2.dev0.

Settings: 10,000 orbits of the innermost planet, 10 Monte Carlo draws, seed 0, at most 300 s per system.

Overall verdicts: stable: 619, marginal: 413, skipped: 35, unstable: 6, inconclusive: 6.

`catalog.csv`, `catalog.parquet` and `catalog_meta.json` are the latest version; files with a date in their name are kept for reference. Column definitions and caveats: https://mimitoz62.github.io/exostab/catalog/
